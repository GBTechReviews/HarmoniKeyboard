# HarmoniKeyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

HarmoniKeyboard respects your privacy.

## Information Collection

HarmoniKeyboard does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information entered through the keyboard.

Text entered using HarmoniKeyboard remains on the user's device.

## Local Storage

HarmoniKeyboard may store the following information locally on the device:

- Keyboard settings
- Theme preferences
- Emoji customisations
- Layout preferences

This information is stored only on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers.

## Third Parties

HarmoniKeyboard does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

## Data Security

All settings and preferences are stored locally on the device.

## Children's Privacy

HarmoniKeyboard does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
